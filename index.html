Key terms

  • 15% off KrisFlyer Saver award rates on selected Scoot flights (vs 30% on Singapore Airlines)
  • Book on flyscoot.com or the Scoot app, logged in with your KrisFlyer account
  • Booking window: 15th to end of the month prior to travel
  • Scoot Economy only — ScootPlus and Super seats excluded
  • Strictly non-cancellable and non-changeable once booked
  • Airport taxes, surcharges and add-ons payable in cash
  • Seats limited, first-come first-served; blackout dates apply on some routes
